**Level:** Beginner

## 🎯 Today's Goal

Learn how to say:

- I, me, you, he, she, we, they
- My, your, his, her, our, their
- This, that, here, there

## 1. Personal Pronouns

Personal pronouns are words we use instead of people's names.

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| I | Naanu |
| Me | Nanage |
| You (informal) | Neenu |
| You (polite) | Neevu |
| He | Avanu |
| She | Avalu |
| It | Adu |
| We | Naavu |
| They | Avaru |

### Useful Sentences

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| I am learning Kannada. | Naanu Kannada kaliyuttiddene. |
| Give me water. | Nanage neeru kodi. |
| He is coming. | Avanu baruttiddane. |
| She is going. | Avalu hoguttiddale. |
| We are going home. | Naavu manege hoguttiddeve. |
| They are coming. | Avaru baruttiddare. |

**Remember:**
- Use *Neevu* when speaking politely to elders, strangers and people you don't know well.
- Use *Neenu* with close friends, children and family members.
- *Nanage* means "to me" or "for me", depending on the sentence.

## 2. Possessive Words – My, Your, His, Her

These words indicate ownership.

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| My | Nanna |
| Your (informal) | Ninna |
| Your (polite) | Nimma |
| His | Avana |
| Her | Avala |
| Its | Adara |
| Our | Namma |
| Their | Avara |

### Useful Sentences

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| My name | Nanna hesaru |
| Your name | Nimma hesaru |
| His house | Avana mane |
| Her book | Avala pustaka |
| Our family | Namma kutumba |
| Their house | Avara mane |

### Try These Sentences

1. Nanna hesaru Ravi. – My name is Ravi.
2. Idu nanna mane. – This is my house.
3. Adu nimma car. – That is your car.
4. Avana hesaru Rahul. – His name is Rahul.
5. Avala hesaru Asha. – Her name is Asha.

## 3. Demonstratives – This, That, Here, There

Use these words to point to people and things or indicate a place.

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| This | Idu |
| That | Adu |
| These | Ivu |
| Those | Avu |
| Here | Illi |
| There | Alli |
| This person (respectful) | Ivaru |
| That person (respectful) | Avaru |

### Useful Sentences

1. Idu enu? – What is this?
2. Adu nanna mane. – That is my house.
3. Illi banni. – Come here.
4. Alli hogi. – Go there.
5. Idu chennagide. – This is nice.
6. Ivaru nanna appa. – This is my father.

## 4. Mini Conversation Practice

**Person A:** Nimma hesaru enu?  
*What is your name?*

**Person B:** Nanna hesaru Ravi.  
*My name is Ravi.*

**Person A:** Idu nimma mane?  
*Is this your house?*

**Person B:** Howdu, idu nanna mane.  
*Yes, this is my house.*

**Person A:** Avaru yaaru?  
*Who is that person?*

**Person B:** Avaru nanna appa.  
*He is my father.*
