Learn commonly used Kannada expressions to make your everyday conversations more natural.

💬 _Useful Expressions:_

- Howdaa? – Is that so?
- Nijavaa? – Really?
- Paravaagilla. – No problem.
- Chintisabedi. – Don't worry.
- Nodona. – Let's see.
- Matte sigona. – Let's meet again.

⏱️ _Duration:_ 15–20 minutes

🎯 _Today's Challenge:_ Use at least 6 expressions from this lesson in a conversation with your family or friends.
