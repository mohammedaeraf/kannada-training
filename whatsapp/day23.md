📚 _Topic: Likes & Dislikes_ ❤️

🔗 _GitHub Lesson:_  
https://github.com/mohammedaeraf/kannada-training/blob/master/day23-likes-dislikes.md

Learn how to express what you like, dislike and ask others about their preferences.

💬 _Useful Expressions:_

- Nanage coffee ishta. – I like coffee.
- Nanage tea ishta illa. – I don't like tea.
- Nimge enu ishta? – What do you like?

⏱️ Duration: 15–20 minutes

🎯 _Today's Challenge:_ Share 3 things you like and 2 things you don't like in Kannada. Ask your family members about their preferences too!
