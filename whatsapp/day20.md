*What you'll learn:*
✅ How to answer a phone call.
✅ How to ask who is calling.
✅ How to ask for someone.
✅ How to say someone is busy or unavailable.
✅ How to ask someone to call back.
✅ How to leave a message.

💬 *Useful Expressions:*

- Yaaru maataduttiddiri? – Who is speaking?
- Ravi iddaara? – Is Ravi there?
- Swalpa kaayiri. – Please wait a little.
- Avaru eega busy iddaare. – He/She is busy now.
- Dayavittu matte kare maadi. – Please call again.
- Naanu nimge nantara kare maaduttene. – I will call you later.

⏱️ *Duration: 15–20 minutes*
