*What will you learn?*

✅ How to talk about yesterday (Ninne).
✅ How to describe what you are doing now.
✅ How to talk about tomorrow (Naale).
✅ How to use past, present and future verb forms.
✅ How to ask questions about past and future activities.

💬 *Some Useful Examples:*

- Naanu ninne market-ge hode. – I went to the market yesterday.
- Naanu eega oota maaduttiddene. – I am eating now.
- Naanu naale market-ge hoguttene. – I will go to the market tomorrow.

⏱️ *Duration: 15–20 minutes*

🎯 *Today's Challenge:*

Describe your activities in three parts:

1️⃣ What did you do yesterday?
2️⃣ What are you doing today?
3️⃣ What will you do tomorrow?

Try to make at least two sentences for each.

Remember, the goal is to start expressing your own thoughts in Kannada rather than just memorising sentences.
