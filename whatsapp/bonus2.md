📚 *Topic: WH-Questions & Everyday Question Words*

Learn how to ask:
- What – Enu
- Who – Yaaru
- Where – Elli
- When – Yaavaga
- Why – Yaake
- How – Hege
- Which – Yaava
- Whose – Yaara

Also learn how to ask about prices, distances and quantities.
