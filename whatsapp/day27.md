📚 _Topic: Small Talk_ 💬

🔗 _Lesson Link:_  
https://github.com/mohammedaeraf/kannada-training/blob/master/day27-small-talk.md

Learn how to start and continue casual conversations with friends, neighbours and people you meet.

💬 _Useful Expressions:_

- Neevu hegiddiri? – How are you?
- Enu samaachaara? – What's the news?
- Naanu chennagiddene. – I am fine.
- Bahala dina aayitu. – It has been a long time.
- Matte bhetiyaagona. – Let's meet again.

⏱️ _Duration:_ 15–20 minutes

🎯 _Today's Challenge:_ Imagine meeting a neighbour after several days. Have a short conversation of 6–8 sentences in Kannada.
