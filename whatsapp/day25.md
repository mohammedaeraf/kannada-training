https://github.com/mohammedaeraf/kannada-training/blob/master/day25-making-requests.md

Learn how to make polite requests in everyday situations.

💬 _Useful Expressions:_

- Neeru kodi. – Please give me water.
- Swalpa kaayiri. – Please wait a little.
- Olage banni. – Please come inside.
- Dayavittu sahaaya maadi. – Please help me.
- Olage barabahuda? – May I come inside?

⏱️ _Duration:_ 15–20 minutes

🎯 _Today's Challenge:_ Make five polite requests in Kannada to your family members. Try speaking without looking at the lesson!
