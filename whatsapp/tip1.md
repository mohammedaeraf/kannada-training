🇮🇳 _A Small Tip for Learning Spoken Kannada_ 🗣️❤️

Namaskara, everyone! 😊

One small suggestion as we continue learning Kannada together.

In everyday conversations, many Kannada speakers commonly use English words such as _door, window, key, car, bike, kitchen, bedroom, fan, light,_ etc., instead of their Kannada equivalents.

So, you don't need to spend time memorising every Kannada noun, especially when the English word is commonly used in daily conversation.

🎯 _Let's focus our learning on what matters most:_
✅ Everyday Kannada sentences and expressions.
✅ Useful verbs and question words.
✅ How to ask questions and respond naturally.
✅ Speaking confidently with native Kannada speakers.
✅ Words and phrases that people actually use in daily conversations.

For example, instead of memorising the Kannada word for _fan_, focus on learning how to say _“Fan haaki”_ (Switch on the fan) or _“Fan off maadi”_ (Switch off the fan).

The goal is **not to learn Kannada vocabulary for the sake of memorising words, but to learn how to communicate naturally in Kannada.**

Of course, do learn Kannada words when they are commonly used or useful to know. 😊

Let's keep it practical, simple, and enjoyable!
