Learn how to talk about your family, occupation, workplace and daily activities in Kannada.

💬 *Useful Expressions:*
- Idu nanna kutumba. – This is my family.
- Naanu shikshaka. – I am a teacher.
- Neevu enu kelasa maaduttiri? – What work do you do?
- Nanna maga oduttiddaane. – My son is studying.

⏱️ *Duration:* 15–20 minutes

🎯 *Today's Challenge:* Introduce your family and occupation in Kannada using 5–6 sentences.
