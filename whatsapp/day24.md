🔗 _GitHub Lesson:_

Learn how to ask for help, explain problems and make simple requests in Kannada.

💬 _Useful Expressions:_

- Nanage sahaaya beku. – I need help.
- Dayavittu sahaaya maadi. – Please help me.
- Nanage gottilla. – I don't know.
- Dayavittu torisi. – Please show me.

⏱️ _Duration:_ 15–20 minutes

🎯 _Today's Challenge:_ Practise asking a family member or friend for help using at least three Kannada sentences.
