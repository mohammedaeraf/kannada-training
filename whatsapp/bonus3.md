📚 *Topic: Everyday Sentence Formation*

Learn how to form:
✅ Positive sentences – I want water.
✅ Negative sentences – I don't want water.
✅ Questions – Do you want water?

You will also practise useful sentence patterns using:
- Beku – Want / Need
- Beda – Don't want
- Beka? – Do you want?

🔗 *Lesson Link:* https://github.com/mohammedaeraf/kannada-training/blob/master/bonus3-positive-negative-question.md

⏱️ *Study time: 10–12 minutes.*

🎯 *Today's Challenge:*
Practise asking your family members what they want and what they don't want – in Kannada!

Remember, the goal is to start creating your own sentences rather than simply memorising them.
