## 🎯 Today's Goal

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Ask who is calling.

- Say that someone is unavailable.
- Ask someone to call back.

## 3. Important Sentence Patterns

### Pattern 1: Asking for Someone

**[Person's name] + iddaara?**

Examples:
- Ravi iddaara? – Is Ravi there?
- Suresh iddaara? – Is Suresh there?

### Pattern 2: Saying Someone Is Busy

**Avaru + eega + busy + iddaare.**

Example:
- Avaru eega busy iddaare. – He/She is busy now.

### Pattern 3: Asking Someone to Call

**Dayavittu + matte + kare maadi.**

Example:
- Dayavittu matte kare maadi. – Please call again.

### Pattern 4: Saying You Will Call Later

**Naanu + nimge + nantara + kare maaduttene.**

Example:
- Naanu nimge nantara kare maaduttene. – I will call you later.

## 4. Mini Conversations

### Conversation 1 – Answering a Phone Call

**Person A:** Hello, yaaru maataduttiddiri?  
*Hello, who is speaking?*

**Person B:** Naanu Ravi maataduttiddene.  
*This is Ravi speaking.*

**Person A:** Namaskara Ravi. Hegiddira?  
*Hello Ravi. How are you?*

**Person B:** Chennagiddini, dhanyavaadagalu. Neevu hegiddira?  
*I am fine, thank you. How are you?*

### Conversation 2 – Asking for Someone

**Person A:** Hello, Suresh iddaara?  
*Hello, is Suresh there?*

**Person B:** Avaru eega busy iddaare.  
*He is busy now.*

**Person A:** Sari, avarige nantara kare maadalu heli.  
*Okay, tell him to call later.*

**Person B:** Sari, heluttene.  
*Okay, I will tell him.*

### Conversation 3 – Calling Back

**Person A:** Kshamisi, naanu eega maatadalu aagalla.  
*Sorry, I cannot talk now.*

**Person B:** Paravaagilla. Nimage nantara kare maaduttene.  
*No problem. I will call you later.*

**Person A:** Sari, dhanyavaadagalu.  
*Okay, thank you.*

### Section A – Choose the Correct Meaning

1. "Matte kare maadi" means:
   - A. Please wait
   - B. Please call again
   - C. Come here

2. "Avaru eega busy iddaare" means:
   - A. They are coming now
   - B. He/She is busy now
   - C. They are at home

3. "Swalpa kaayiri" means:
   - A. Please wait a little

4. "Naanu Ravi maataduttiddene" means:
