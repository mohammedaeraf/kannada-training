# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Week 1 Assessment
## Answer Key with Questions

Congratulations to everyone who participated in the Week 1 Assessment! 🎉

This answer key covers the MCQs and translation questions from Day 1 to Day 6.

## Section A: Multiple-Choice Questions (30 Marks)

**Q1. What does “Namaskara” mean?**

Answer: Hello.

**Q2. What does “Howdu” mean?**

Answer: Yes.

**Q3. What does “Dhanyavaadagalu” mean?**

Answer: Thank you.

**Q4. What does “Nanage neeru beku” mean?**

Answer: I want water.

**Q5. What is the Kannada word for mother?**

Answer: Amma.

**Q6. Which Kannada number means 2?**

Answer: Eradu.

**Q7. What does “Gottilla” mean?**

Answer: I don't know.

**Q8. What does “Banni” mean?**

Answer: Please come.

**Q9. What does “Nimma hesaru enu?” mean?**

Answer: What is your name?

**Q10. How do you say “I don't want coffee” in Kannada?**

Answer: Nanage kaafi beda.

**Q11. What does “Idu eshtu?” mean?**

Answer: How much is this?

**Q12. Which Kannada number means 10?**

Answer: Hattu.

**Q13. What does “Ivaru yaaru?” mean?**

Answer: Who is this person?

**Q14. What does “Dayavittu nidhaanavaagi maatadi” mean?**

Answer: Please speak slowly.

**Q15. What does “Naanu manege hoguttiddene” mean?**

Answer: I am going home.

**Q16. Which phrase means “Please give me water”?**

Answer: Dayavittu neeru kodi.

**Q17. What does “Paravaagilla” mean?**

Answer: No problem.

**Q18. A shopkeeper asks, “Enu beku?” What does this mean?**

Answer: What would you like?

**Q19. Someone asks, “Neevu hegiddira?” What is the most suitable response?**

Answer: Chennagiddini, dhanyavaadagalu. (I'm fine, thank you.)

**Q20. What does “Naanu Kannada kaliyuttiddene” mean?**

Answer: I am learning Kannada.

**Q21. What is the Kannada phrase for “I want three”?**

Answer: Nanage mooru beku.

**Q22. What is the Kannada word for an older sister?**

Answer: Akka.

**Q23. Someone asks, “Neevu enu maaduttiddiri?” What are they asking?**

Answer: What are you doing?

**Q24. What does “Naanu neeru kudiyuttiddene” mean?**

Answer: I am drinking water.

**Q25. Complete the conversation.**

Person A: Nimage neeru beka?

Person B: Howdu, dayavittu neeru ______.

Answer: Kodi.

**Q26. What does “Bere enadaru beka?” mean?**

Answer: Do you need anything else?

**Q27. How do you politely ask someone to sit in Kannada?**

Answer: Kulithukolli.

**Q28. How do you say “I don't understand” in Kannada?**

Answer: Nanage artha aagalla.

**Q29. How would you answer “Nimma hesaru enu?” if your name is Ravi?**

Answer: Nanna hesaru Ravi.

**Q30. How do you say “I want five bananas” in Kannada?**

Answer: Nanage aidu baalehannu beku.
