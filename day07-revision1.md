## 2. Sentence Pattern Revision

Learn these patterns. You can use them to make many sentences.

### Pattern 1: Introducing yourself

**Nanna hesaru + name**

- Nanna hesaru Aeraf. — My name is Aeraf.
- Nanna hesaru Ravi. — My name is Ravi.

### Pattern 2: Saying what you need

**Nanage + thing + beku**

- Nanage neeru beku. — I want water.
- Nanage kaafi beku. — I want coffee.
- Nanage sahaaya beku. — I need help.

### Pattern 3: Saying what you don't want

**Nanage + thing + beda**

- Nanage kaafi beda. — I don't want coffee.
- Nanage sakkare beda. — I don't want sugar.

### Pattern 4: Asking a question

- Idu enu? — What is this?
- Ivaru yaaru? — Who is this person?
- Idu eshtu? — How much is this?
- Neevu hegiddira? — How are you?
- Nimma hesaru enu? — What is your name?

### Pattern 5: Talking about an activity

- Naanu oota maaduttiddene. — I am eating.
- Naanu neeru kudiyuttiddene. — I am drinking water.
- Naanu manege hoguttiddene. — I am going home.
- Naanu Kannada kaliyuttiddene. — I am learning Kannada.

## 3. Speaking Practice – Everyday Situations

### Conversation A: Meeting Someone

**Person A:** Namaskara! Neevu hegiddira?  
*Hello! How are you?*

**Person B:** Chennagiddini, dhanyavaadagalu. Neevu?  
*I'm fine, thank you. And you?*

**Person A:** Naanu chennagiddini. Nimma hesaru enu?  
*I'm fine. What is your name?*

**Person B:** Nanna hesaru Ravi.  
*My name is Ravi.*

### Conversation B: At a Shop

**Shopkeeper:** Namaskara! Enu beku?  
*Hello! What would you like?*

**Customer:** Nanage neeru beku.  
*I want water.*

**Shopkeeper:** Bere enadaru beka?  
*Do you need anything else?*

**Customer:** Beda, dhanyavaadagalu.  
*No, thank you.*

**Shopkeeper:** Sari.  
*Okay.*

### Conversation C: At Home

**Person A:** Neevu enu maaduttiddira?  
*What are you doing?*

**Person B:** Naanu oota maaduttiddene.  
*I'm eating.*

**Person A:** Nimge neeru beka?  
*Would you like some water?*

**Person B:** Howdu, dayavittu neeru kodi.  
*Yes, please give me water.*

## 5. Family Speaking Challenge – 10 Minutes

Do this activity with a family member.

**Round 1 – Introduction**

Introduce yourself in Kannada using at least three sentences.

**Round 2 – Questions**

Ask your partner these questions:

1. Nimma hesaru enu?
2. Neevu hegiddira?
3. Idu enu?
4. Idu eshtu?
5. Neevu enu maaduttiddira?

**Round 3 – Role-play**

Pretend you are buying something from a shop. Ask for water, ask the price, and thank the shopkeeper.

**Round 4 – Numbers**

Count from one to ten in Kannada without looking at the table.

## 🏠 Homework

- Revise the lessons from Days 1–6.
- Practise the three conversations aloud.
- Write five Kannada sentences using this week's vocabulary.
- Record a one-minute voice note introducing yourself and talking about your family or daily routine.
