## 🎯 Today's Goal

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- Ask for items in a shop.

- Understand common shopkeeper questions.
- Request a different item or quantity.
- Have a simple shopping conversation in Kannada.

## 3. Important Sentence Patterns

### Pattern A: Asking for an item

**Nanage + item + beku** = I want / need an item.

Examples:
- Nanage shirt beku. — I want a shirt.
- Nanage hannu beku. — I want fruit.
- Nanage ondu cheela beku. — I want one bag.

### Pattern B: Asking whether something is available

**Item + ideya?** = Is the item available? / Is there an item?

Examples:
- Haalu ideya? — Is milk available?
- Bere banna ideya? — Is another colour available?
- Doddadu ideya? — Is a bigger one available?

### Pattern C: Asking to see something

**Bere + item + torisi** = Please show another item.

Examples:
- Bere shirt torisi. — Please show another shirt.
- Bere banna torisi. — Please show another colour.
- Chikkadu torisi. — Please show a smaller one.

## 4. Mini Conversation: At a Clothing Shop 👕

**Shopkeeper:** Namaskara! Enu beku?  
*Hello! What would you like?*

**Customer:** Nanage ondu shirt beku.  
*I want a shirt.*

**Shopkeeper:** Yaava banna beku?  
*Which colour would you like?*

**Customer:** Neeli banna beku.  
*I want blue.*

**Shopkeeper:** Idu nodi.  
*Have a look at this.*

**Customer:** Bere shirt torisi.  
*Please show me another shirt.*

**Shopkeeper:** Sari, idu hegide?  
*Okay, how is this one?*

**Customer:** Idu chennagide. Idanne kodi.  
*This is nice. Give me this one.*

**Shopkeeper:** Sari!  
*Okay!*

## 6. Speaking Practice with Family 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Take turns playing the customer and shopkeeper.

Practise asking for:

1. A shirt in a different colour.
2. A smaller bag.
3. One more banana.
4. A different item.
5. An item you like.

Use these sentences:

- Nanage ondu shirt beku. — I want a shirt.
- Bere banna torisi. — Please show another colour.
- Chikkadu ideya? — Is a smaller one available?
- Innondu kodi. — Please give me another one.
- Idu nanage ishta. — I like this.
