# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Day 22
## Past, Present & Future (ಭೂತಕಾಲ, ವರ್ತಮಾನಕಾಲ ಮತ್ತು ಭವಿಷ್ಯತ್‌ಕಾಲ)

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 15–20 minutes  
**Course:** Spoken Kannada for Beginners

## 🎯 Today's Goal

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

1. Talk about things you did in the past.
2. Describe what you are doing now.
3. Talk about things you will do in the future.
4. Use yesterday, today and tomorrow in conversations.

## 1. Three Important Time Words

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| Yesterday | Ninne |
| Today | Indu |
| Tomorrow | Naale |
| Now | Eega |
| Later | Nantara |
| Every day | Pratidina |
| In the morning | Belagge |
| In the evening | Sanje |

## 2. Understanding the Three Tenses

Let's use the verb **Hogu (ಹೋಗು) – To go**.

| Tense | English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Past | I went. | Naanu hode. |
| Present | I am going. | Naanu hoguttiddene. |
| Future | I will go. | Naanu hoguttene. |

Notice how the verb changes:

- Hode – Went
- Hoguttiddene – Am going
- Hoguttene – Will go / Usually go

## 4. Using Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow

These words make it easier to communicate when something happened or will happen.

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| I went to the market yesterday. | Naanu ninne market-ge hode. |
| I am going to the market today. | Naanu indu market-ge hoguttiddene. |
| I will go to the market tomorrow. | Naanu naale market-ge hoguttene. |
| I ate yesterday. | Naanu ninne oota maadide. |
| I am eating now. | Naanu eega oota maaduttiddene. |
| I will eat later. | Naanu nantara oota maaduttene. |
| I came yesterday. | Naanu ninne bande. |
| I am coming now. | Naanu eega baruttiddene. |
| I will come tomorrow. | Naanu naale baruttene. |

## 6. Mini Conversation – Talking About Yesterday and Tomorrow

**Person A:** Neevu ninne ellige hogiddiri?  
*Where did you go yesterday?*

**Person B:** Naanu market-ge hogidde.  
*I went to the market.*

**Person A:** Eega enu maaduttiddiri?  
*What are you doing now?*

**Person B:** Naanu oota maaduttiddene.  
*I am eating.*

**Person A:** Naale ellige hoguttiri?  
*Where will you go tomorrow?*

**Person B:** Naanu office-ge hoguttene.  
*I will go to the office.*

## 7. Quick Quiz

## ⭐ Remember

| Time | Kannada |
|---|---|
| Yesterday | Ninne |
| Today | Indu |
| Tomorrow | Naale |
| I went | Naanu hode |
| I am going | Naanu hoguttiddene |
| I will go | Naanu hoguttene |

**Important:** The form *hoguttene* can mean "I will go" or "I usually go", depending on context. Words like *naale* (tomorrow) and *pratidina* (every day) help make the intended meaning clear.
