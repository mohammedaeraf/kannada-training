# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Day 06
## Useful Everyday Verbs – Come, Go, Eat, Drink and More

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 15–20 minutes  
**Course:** Spoken Kannada for Beginners

## 🎯 Today's Goal

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- Recognise common Kannada verbs used in everyday conversations.
- Make simple sentences about daily activities.
- Say what you are doing and ask others what they are doing.

## 2. Useful Everyday Sentences

### 1. I am coming.

**Kannada:** ನಾನು ಬರುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ.  
**Pronunciation:** Naanu baruttiddene.  
**Meaning:** I am coming.

### 2. I am going home.

**Kannada:** ನಾನು ಮನೆಗೆ ಹೋಗುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ.  
**Pronunciation:** Naanu manege hoguttiddene.  
**Meaning:** I am going home.

### 3. I am eating.

**Kannada:** ನಾನು ಊಟ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ.  
**Pronunciation:** Naanu oota maaduttiddene.  
**Meaning:** I am eating / having a meal.

### 4. I am drinking water.

**Kannada:** ನಾನು ನೀರು ಕುಡಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ.  
**Pronunciation:** Naanu neeru kudiyuttiddene.  
**Meaning:** I am drinking water.

### 5. Speak slowly, please.

**Kannada:** ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಮಾತಾಡಿ.  
**Pronunciation:** Dayavittu nidhaanavaagi maatadi.  
**Meaning:** Please speak slowly.

### 6. Look here.

**Kannada:** ಇಲ್ಲಿ ನೋಡಿ.  
**Pronunciation:** Illi nodi.  
**Meaning:** Look here.

### 7. Please give me water.

**Kannada:** ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ನೀರು ಕೊಡಿ.  
**Pronunciation:** Dayavittu neeru kodi.  
**Meaning:** Please give me water.

### 8. I don't understand.

**Kannada:** ನನಗೆ ಅರ್ಥ ಆಗುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ.  
**Pronunciation:** Nanage artha aaguttilla.  
**Meaning:** I don't understand.

### 9. What are you doing?

**Kannada:** ನೀವು ಏನು ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಿ?  
**Pronunciation:** Neevu enu maaduttiddiri?  
**Meaning:** What are you doing?

### 10. I am studying Kannada.

**Kannada:** ನಾನು ಕನ್ನಡ ಕಲಿಯುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ.  
**Pronunciation:** Naanu Kannada kaliyuttiddene.  
**Meaning:** I am learning Kannada.

## 3. Simple Sentence Patterns

### Pattern A: I am doing something

**Structure:** Naanu + activity + maaduttiddene

Examples:

- **Naanu kelasa maaduttiddene.** — I am working.
- **Naanu oota maaduttiddene.** — I am eating.
- **Naanu vyaayaama maaduttiddene.** — I am exercising.

### Pattern B: I want something

**Structure:** Nanage + thing + beku

Examples:

- **Nanage neeru beku.** — I want water.
- **Nanage kaafi beku.** — I want coffee.
- **Nanage sahaaya beku.** — I need help.

### Pattern C: Please do something

Use **-i** or the appropriate polite verb form when making a polite request. Learn these common expressions as complete phrases:

- **Banni** — Please come.
- **Hogi** — Please go.
- **Koodi** — Please sit.
- **Nodi** — Please look.
- **Kodi** — Please give.
- **Heli** — Please tell / say.

## 4. Mini Conversation – At Home

**Person A:** ನೀವು ಏನು ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದೀರಿ?  
**Pronunciation:** Neevu enu maaduttiddiri?  
**Meaning:** What are you doing?

**Person B:** ನಾನು ಊಟ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಿದ್ದೇನೆ.  
**Pronunciation:** Naanu oota maaduttiddene.  
**Meaning:** I am eating.

**Person A:** ನಿಮಗೆ ನೀರು ಬೇಕಾ?  
**Pronunciation:** Nimage neeru beka?  
**Meaning:** Do you want water?

**Person B:** ಹೌದು, ನೀರು ಕೊಡಿ.  
**Pronunciation:** Howdu, neeru kodi.  
**Meaning:** Yes, please give me water.

**Person A:** ಸರಿ.  
**Pronunciation:** Sari.  
**Meaning:** Okay.

**Person B:** ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು.  
**Pronunciation:** Dhanyavaadagalu.  
**Meaning:** Thank you.

## 5. Speaking Practice

Complete these sentences aloud using the suggested words.

1. Naanu ______ maaduttiddene. — I am ______.
   - *oota* (eating / having a meal)
   - *kelasa* (work)
   - *vyaayaama* (exercise)

2. Nanage ______ beku. — I want ______.
   - *neeru* (water)
   - *kaafi* (coffee)
   - *sahaaya* (help)

3. Ask a family member: **Neevu enu maaduttiddiri?** — What are you doing?

4. Make polite requests using **Banni, Koodi, Nodi,** and **Kodi**.
