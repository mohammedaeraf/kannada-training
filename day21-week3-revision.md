## 3. Speaking Test – 50 Marks

Complete all five speaking activities.

### Task 1 – Auto / Taxi Conversation (10 Marks)

**Situation:** You want to go to the market by auto.

Speak to the driver in Kannada. Include:

- Ask whether the driver will go to the market.
- Ask about the fare.
- Request the driver to go slowly.
- Give a direction.
- Ask the driver to stop.

Useful expressions:

- Market-ge hoguttira?
- Baadige eshtu?
- Swalpa nidhaanavaagi hogi.
- Balakke tirugi.
- Illi nillisi.

### Task 2 – Restaurant Conversation (10 Marks)

**Situation:** You visit a restaurant with your family.

Speak to the waiter in Kannada. Include:

- Ask for the menu.
- Order dosa.
- Ask for water.
- Say that you don't want spicy food.
- Ask for the bill.

Useful expressions:

- Menu kodi.
- Nanage dose beku.
- Swalpa neeru kodi.
- Nanage khaara beda.
- Bill kodi.

### Task 3 – Vegetable Shopping (10 Marks)

**Situation:** You visit a vegetable shop.

Include:

- Ask for tomatoes.
- Ask the price of one kilogram.
- Ask for half a kilogram.
- Ask for another vegetable.
- Ask for a bag.

Useful expressions:

- Tomato ideya?
- Ondu kilo eshtu?
- Ardha kilo kodi.
- Bere tarakaari kodi.
- Cheela kodi.

### Task 4 – Neighbour & Apartment Conversation (10 Marks)

**Situation:** You meet a new neighbour near your apartment.

Introduce yourself and include:

- Ask their name.
- Tell them where you live.
- Ask about their family.
- Invite them inside.
- Offer help.

Useful expressions:

- Nimma hesaru enu?
- Naanu nimma pakkada maneyavaru.
- Neevu illi vaasisuttira?
- Olage banni.
- Enadaru sahaaya beka?

### Task 5 – Phone Conversation (10 Marks)

**Situation:** Someone calls and asks to speak to your family member.

Include:

- Answer the phone.
- Ask who is speaking.
- Say the person is busy.
- Ask the caller to wait.
- Tell the caller that the person will call later.

Useful expressions:

- Hello, yaaru maataduttiddiri?
- Avaru eega busy iddaare.
- Swalpa kaayiri.
- Avarige heli.
- Avaru nimage nantara kare maaduttare.

### Suggested Evaluation Criteria

For each speaking activity, assess:

- Correct use of Kannada expressions – 4 marks
- Sentence formation – 2 marks
- Pronunciation and clarity – 2 marks
- Confidence and fluency – 2 marks

Don't worry about minor pronunciation or Roman spelling variations. Focus on communicating the correct meaning.

## 🏆 Week 3 Speaking Challenge

Try to complete all five conversations without looking at the lesson.

**Your target:** Speak at least 5–6 sentences in each situation.

Remember:

- Don't worry about making mistakes.
- Don't translate every word from English.
- Use simple Kannada sentences.
- Practise aloud, not just by reading.

**The real achievement is being able to communicate!**
