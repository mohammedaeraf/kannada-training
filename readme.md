# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada for Beginners
## A 30-Day Kannada Speaking Challenge

Welcome to our family Kannada-learning journey!

This free, beginner-friendly course is designed to help us learn **basic spoken Kannada in 30 days**, through simple daily lessons, practical sentences, pronunciation guides, and conversation practice.

No prior knowledge of Kannada is required. We will primarily focus on speaking and understanding Kannada, rather than learning to read or write the script.

## 🎯 Our Goal

By the end of 30 days, we aim to:

- Greet people and introduce ourselves in Kannada.
- Speak basic Kannada while shopping and ordering food.
- Communicate with auto drivers, neighbours, and shopkeepers.
- Ask simple questions and understand common responses.
- Participate in short everyday conversations.

## 🕒 How to Learn

Try to spend 15–20 minutes every day.

1. Read the Kannada words and their English pronunciation.
2. Speak every sentence aloud at least three times.
3. Practise the mini-dialogue with a family member.
4. Complete the daily quiz.
5. Use at least one new Kannada sentence in a real conversation.

## 💡 Learning Tips

- Don't worry about making mistakes.
- Focus on speaking confidently rather than speaking perfectly.
- Revise previous lessons regularly.
- Encourage every family member to participate.
- Be consistent—even 15 minutes a day makes a difference.
