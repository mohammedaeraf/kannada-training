## 3. Important Sentence Patterns

### Pattern A: Saying what you want

**Nanage + item + beku** = I want / need something.

Examples:

- Nanage haalu beku. — I want milk.
- Nanage idli beku. — I want idli.
- Nanage hannu beku. — I want fruit.

### Pattern B: Saying what you don't want

**Nanage + item + beda** = I don't want something.

Examples:

- Nanage kaafi beda. — I don't want coffee.
- Nanage sakkare beda. — I don't want sugar.
- Nanage khaara beda. — I don't want spicy food.

### Pattern C: Asking for a little

**Swalpa + item + kodi** = Please give me some...

Examples:

- Swalpa neeru kodi. — Please give me some water.
- Swalpa haalu kodi. — Please give me some milk.

### Saying the time

For basic conversation, say the hour followed by **gante**.

- 6:00 — Aaru gante.
- 7:00 — Elu gante.
- 8:00 — Entu gante.
- 10:00 — Hattu gante.
- 12:00 — Hanneradu gante.

To say _at_ a particular hour, add **-ge**:

- Aaru gantege — At six o'clock.
- Elu gantege — At seven o'clock.
- Hattu gantege — At ten o'clock.

## 4. Important Sentence Patterns

### Pattern A: Talking about your routine

**Naanu + activity + maaduttene** = I do an activity.

Examples:

- Naanu snaana maaduttene. — I take a bath.
- Naanu oota maaduttene. — I eat / have a meal.
- Naanu kelasa maaduttene. — I work.

### Pattern B: Saying when you do something

**Time + activity**

Examples:

- Belagge aaru gantege eluttene. — I wake up at six in the morning.
- Madhyahna ondu gantege oota maaduttene. — I eat lunch at one in the afternoon.
- Raatri hattu gantege malaguttene. — I go to bed at ten at night.

### Pattern C: Asking when someone does something

**Neevu yaavaga + activity?**

Examples:

- Neevu yaavaga baruttiri? — When will you come?
- Neevu yaavaga hoguttiri? — When will you go?
- Neevu yaavaga oota maaduttiri? — When do you eat?
