# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Day 30

## Final Speaking Challenge (ಅಂತಿಮ ಮಾತಿನ ಸವಾಲು)

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 20–25 minutes  
**Course:** 30-Day Spoken Kannada for Beginners

### 🎯 Today's Goal

Use the Kannada vocabulary, expressions and sentence patterns learned throughout the course to handle everyday conversations confidently.

Today is about speaking, not memorising!

### 2. Challenge 1 – Introduce Yourself

Speak for 1–2 minutes in Kannada.

Include:

1. Your name
2. Where you are from
3. Where you live
4. Your occupation
5. Your family
6. Your hobbies
7. Why you are learning Kannada

**Example:**

Namaskaara! Nanna hesaru Ravi.

Naanu Bhatkal-davanu.

Naanu iga Bengaluru-nalli vaasisuttiddene.

Naanu office-nalli kelasa maaduttene.

Nanage ibbaru makkalu iddaare.

Nanage swimming mattu cricket ishta.

Naanu Kannada kaliyuttiddene.

### 3. Challenge 2 – Everyday Situations

Practise these five real-life situations.

**Situation A: At a Restaurant**

- Menu kodi. – Please give me the menu.
- Nanage dose beku. – I want dosa.
- Swalpa neeru kodi. – Please give me some water.
- Bill kodi. – Please give me the bill.

**Situation B: At a Shop**

- Idara bele eshtu? – How much does this cost?
- Bere banna ideya? – Is another colour available?
- Swalpa kadime maadi. – Please reduce the price.
- Sari, idannu kodi. – Okay, give me this.

**Situation C: Travelling by Auto**

- Market-ge hogabeku. – I need to go to the market.
- Baadige eshtu? – How much is the fare?
- Swalpa nidhaanavaagi hogi. – Please go slowly.
- Illi nillisi. – Stop here.

**Situation D: Talking to a Neighbour**

- Namaskaara, hegiddira? – Hello, how are you?
- Nimma hesaru enu? – What is your name?
- Neevu illi vaasisuttira? – Do you live here?
- Manege banni. – Please come home.

**Situation E: On the Phone**

- Hello, yaaru maataduttiddiri? – Hello, who is speaking?
- Ravi iddaara? – Is Ravi there?
- Avaru eega busy iddaare. – He/She is busy now.
- Dayavittu matte kare maadi. – Please call again.

### 4. Challenge 3 – Translate Without Looking

Translate these sentences into Kannada using English letters.

1. I am going home.
2. I need some water.
3. What is your name?
4. How much does this cost?
5. Please speak slowly.
6. I don't know.
7. I will come tomorrow.
8. Please bring coffee.
9. Where is the hospital?
10. I am learning Kannada.

### 5. Challenge 4 – Complete Conversation

Imagine you meet a new person and have a conversation.

Try to include:

- Greetings and introductions
- Asking about family
- Talking about work
- Asking for directions
- Making a request
- Saying goodbye

Speak at least 10–12 sentences without referring to the lesson.

### 🏆 Your 30-Day Achievement

Congratulations! You have completed the 30-day beginner Kannada course.

You have practised:

- Greetings and introductions
- Numbers, family and everyday vocabulary
- Questions and sentence formation
- Shopping, food and restaurant conversations
- Directions and travelling
- Neighbour and apartment conversations
- Phone conversations

**Remember:** The goal is not perfect Kannada. The goal is to communicate confidently.

### 🚀 What Next?

Continue practising for 10–15 minutes every day.

1. Speak at least five Kannada sentences daily.
2. Talk to Kannada-speaking friends and neighbours.
3. Watch Kannada videos with subtitles.
4. Try ordering food and shopping in Kannada.
5. Learn five new words every week.
