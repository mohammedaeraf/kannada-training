# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Day 10

## Money & Prices 💰

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 15–20 minutes  
**Course:** Spoken Kannada for Beginners

---

## 🎯 Today's Goal

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- Ask the price of an item.
- Understand common price-related questions.
- Ask for a lower price politely.
- Understand basic money-related expressions.
- Have a simple conversation while buying something.

## 4. Important Sentence Patterns

### Pattern A: Asking the price

**Idara bele eshtu?** = What is its price?

Examples:

- Idara bele eshtu? — What is its price?
- Ee shirt bele eshtu? — What is the price of this shirt?
- Ee hannu eshtu? — How much is this fruit?

### Pattern B: Asking for a discount

**Swalpa kadime maadi** = Please reduce it a little.

Examples:

- Swalpa kadime maadi. — Please reduce the price a little.
- Innu kadime maadi. — Please reduce it a little more.
- Tumba dubaari ide. — It is very expensive.

### Pattern C: Saying you will buy something

**Naanu + item + tegedukolluttene** = I will take / buy the item.

Examples:

- Naanu idannu tegedukolluttene. — I will take this.
- Sari, idannu tegedukolluttene. — Okay, I'll take this.

## 5. Mini Conversation: At a Shop 🛍️

**Customer:** Ee shirt bele eshtu?  
_What is the price of this shirt?_

**Shopkeeper:** Ainooru roopayi.  
_Five hundred rupees._

**Customer:** Tumba dubaari ide. Swalpa kadime maadi.  
_It's very expensive. Please reduce the price a little._

**Shopkeeper:** Naalkunooru aivattu roopayi kodi.  
_Pay four hundred and fifty rupees._

**Customer:** Naalkunooru roopayige kodtira?  
_Will you give it for four hundred rupees?_

**Shopkeeper:** Sari, naalkunooru roopayi kodi.  
_Okay, pay four hundred rupees._

**Customer:** Sari, idannu tegedukolluttene.  
_Okay, I'll take this._

## 6. Speaking Practice with Family 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Take turns playing the customer and shopkeeper.

Practise these situations:

1. A shirt costs ₹500. Ask for a discount.
2. A bag costs ₹1,000. Say that it is expensive.
3. Ask whether the shopkeeper has change for ₹500.
4. Ask for the bill.
5. Agree to buy an item.

Useful sentences:

- Idara bele eshtu? — What is its price?
- Swalpa kadime maadi. — Please reduce it a little.
- Tumba dubaari ide. — It is very expensive.
- Chillare ideya? — Do you have change?
- Bill kodi. — Please give me the bill.
- Sari, idannu tegedukolluttene. — Okay, I'll take this.
