# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Day 14
## Week 2 Conversation Practice 🗣️

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 15–20 minutes  
**Course:** Spoken Kannada for Beginners

---

## 🎯 Today's Goal

Today, we will revise and practise Kannada through everyday conversations.

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Talk about food and drinks.
- Shop for items and ask about prices.
- Make simple requests at home.
- Talk about your daily routine.
- Ask for directions.

**Today's focus:** Speak aloud rather than simply reading the sentences.

## 2. Conversation 1: At a Restaurant 🍽️

**Waiter:** Namaskara! Enu beku?  
*Hello! What would you like?*

**Customer:** Nanage dose beku.  
*I want dosa.*

**Waiter:** Kaafi beka?  
*Would you like coffee?*

**Customer:** Beda, neeru kodi.  
*No, please give me water.*

**Waiter:** Sari. Bere enadaru beka?  
*Okay. Would you like anything else?*

**Customer:** Beda, dhanyavaadagalu.  
*No, thank you.*

### Your Turn

Practise again, replacing *dose* with *idli* or *oota*, and *neeru* with *kaafi* or *haalu*.

## 3. Conversation 2: Shopping for a Shirt 🛍️

**Customer:** Ee shirt bele eshtu?  
*What is the price of this shirt?*

**Shopkeeper:** Ainooru roopayi.  
*Five hundred rupees.*

**Customer:** Tumba dubaari ide. Swalpa kadime maadi.  
*It is very expensive. Please reduce the price a little.*

**Shopkeeper:** Naalkunooru aivattu roopayi.  
*Four hundred and fifty rupees.*

**Customer:** Sari, idannu tegedukolluttene.  
*Okay, I'll take this.*

### Your Turn

Repeat the conversation using these prices:

- ₹100 — Nooru roopayi
- ₹200 — Innooru roopayi
- ₹500 — Ainooru roopayi

Try asking for a discount each time.

## 4. Conversation 3: At Home 🏠

**Person A:** Keeli elli ide?  
*Where is the key?*

**Person B:** Mejina mele ide.  
*It is on the table.*

**Person A:** Neeru taruttira?  
*Will you bring water?*

**Person B:** Howdu, taruttene.  
*Yes, I will bring it.*

**Person A:** Dhanyavaadagalu!  
*Thank you!*

**Person B:** Paravaagilla.  
*No problem.*

### Your Turn

Replace *keeli* (key) with *remote* or *phone*. Practise asking where each item is.

## 5. Conversation 4: Talking About Your Routine ⏰

**Person A:** Neevu belagge eshtu gantege eluttiri?  
*What time do you wake up in the morning?*

**Person B:** Naanu aaru gantege eluttēne.  
*I wake up at six o'clock.*

**Person A:** Neevu yaavaga oota maaduttiri?  
*When do you eat?*

**Person B:** Naanu madhyahna ondu gantege oota maaduttene.  
*I eat lunch at one o'clock in the afternoon.*

**Person A:** Chennagide!  
*That's good!*

### Your Turn

Talk about your own routine. Use your actual waking-up time, meal times, and bedtime.

## 6. Conversation 5: Asking for Directions 🧭

**Person A:** Kshamisi, bus nildana ellide?  
*Excuse me, where is the bus stop?*

**Person B:** Neravaagi hogi.  
*Go straight.*

**Person A:** Aamele?  
*Then what?*

**Person B:** Balakke tirugi. Bus nildana allide.  
*Turn right. The bus stop is there.*

**Person A:** Tumba dhanyavaadagalu!  
*Thank you very much!*

**Person B:** Paravaagilla.  
*No problem.*

### Your Turn

Replace *bus nildana* with *angadi* (shop) or *asp atre* (hospital). Give directions using *neravaagi*, *edakke*, and *balakke*.

## 7. Quick Speaking Challenge 🎯

Try to answer each question in Kannada without looking at the answers.

1. **Nimage enu beku?** — What would you like?
2. **Idara bele eshtu?** — What is its price?
3. **Keeli elli ide?** — Where is the key?
4. **Neevu eshtu gantege eluttiri?** — What time do you wake up?
5. **Bus nildana ellide?** — Where is the bus stop?

Suggested answers:

- Nanage neeru beku. — I want water.
- Ainooru roopayi. — Five hundred rupees.
- Mejina mele ide. — It is on the table.
- Naanu aaru gantege eluttēne. — I wake up at six.
- Neravaagi hogi. — Go straight.

These are examples; you can give different answers that fit the question.

## 8. Family Speaking Game 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Play in pairs. One person asks a question, and the other responds in Kannada. Then swap roles.

**Round 1:** Order food or drinks.

**Round 2:** Buy an item and negotiate its price.

**Round 3:** Find a missing item at home.

**Round 4:** Describe your daily routine.

**Round 5:** Ask for directions to a nearby place.

Give one point for each conversation completed without reading the English translation.

## 9. Homework 🏠

3. Learn five sentences that you can use in real life.
4. Record a one-minute Kannada conversation with a family member, if everyone is comfortable.
5. Help one another catch up before moving to the next lesson.
