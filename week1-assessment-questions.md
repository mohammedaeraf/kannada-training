## 🔐 Answer Key – Additional Questions

| Question | Correct Answer | Explanation |
|---:|---|---|
| 26 | B – Do you need anything else? | “Bere” means other/additional; “beka?” asks whether something is needed. |
| 27 | C – Kulithukolli | A polite expression meaning “Please sit down.” |
| 28 | B – Nanage artha aagalla | Means “I don't understand.” |
| 29 | B – Nanna hesaru Ravi | This directly answers “What is your name?” |
| 30 | A – Nanage aidu baalehannu beku | “Aidu” means five, “baalehannu” means banana, and “beku” means want/need. |
