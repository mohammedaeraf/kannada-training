## 3. Important Sentence Patterns

### Pattern A: Asking where something is

**Item + elli ide?** = Where is the item?

Examples:

- Keeli elli ide? — Where is the key?
- Neeru elli ide? — Where is the water?
- Remote elli ide? — Where is the remote?

### Pattern B: Saying where something is

**Item + place + alli ide** = The item is at/in the place.

Examples:

- Neeru adugemaneyalli ide. — The water is in the kitchen.
- Keeli mejina mele ide. — The key is on the table.
- Batte roominalli ide. — The clothes are in the room.

### Pattern C: Giving polite instructions

Examples:

- Illi idi. — Put it here.
- Baagilu tereyiri. — Please open the door.
- Baagilu muchchi. — Please close the door.
- Swalpa kaayiri. — Please wait a little.

## 4. Mini Conversation: Finding the Key 🔑

**Person A:** Keeli elli ide?  
_Where is the key?_

**Person B:** Mejina mele ide.  
_It is on the table._

**Person A:** Nanage kaanuttilla.  
_I can't see it._

**Person B:** Alli nodi.  
_Look there._

**Person A:** Aah, illide! Dhanyavaadagalu.  
_Ah, here it is! Thank you._

**Person B:** Paravaagilla.  
_No problem._

## 6. Speaking Practice with Family 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Take turns asking and answering these questions:

1. **Keeli elli ide?** — Where is the key?
2. **Neeru elli ide?** — Where is the water?
3. **Remote elli ide?** — Where is the remote?
4. **Baagilu muchchuttira?** — Will you close the door?
5. **Light haakuttira?** — Will you switch on the light?

Practise answering with:

- Mejina mele ide. — It is on the table.
- Adu adugemaneyalli ide. — It is in the kitchen.
- Howdu, maaduttene. — Yes, I will.
- Sari. — Okay.
- Illi idi. — Put it here.
