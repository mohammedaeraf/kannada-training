**Level:** Beginner

## 🎯 Today's Goal

Learn to ask everyday questions in Kannada using:

- What, Who, Where
- When, Why, How
- Which, Whose
- How much, How many

## 1. Basic WH-Question Words

| English | Kannada | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| What | ಏನು | Enu |
| Who | ಯಾರು | Yaaru |
| Where | ಎಲ್ಲಿ | Elli |
| When | ಯಾವಾಗ | Yaavaga |
| Why | ಯಾಕೆ | Yaake |
| How | ಹೇಗೆ | Hege |

### Useful Examples

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| What is this? | Idu enu? |
| Who is he? | Avanu yaaru? |
| Where are you? | Neevu elli iddiri? |
| When will you come? | Neevu yaavaga baruttiri? |
| Why are you late? | Neevu yaake tadavaagi bandiri? |
| How are you? | Neevu hegiddeera? |

## 2. Which, Whose, How Much & How Many

| English | Kannada | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Which | ಯಾವ | Yaava |
| Which one? | ಯಾವುದು | Yaavudu |
| Whose | ಯಾರ | Yaara |
| How much? | ಎಷ್ಟು | Eshtu |
| How many? | ಎಷ್ಟು | Eshtu |
| From where? | ಎಲ್ಲಿಂದ | Ellinda |
| To where? | ಎಲ್ಲಿಗೆ | Ellige |

### Useful Examples

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| Which colour do you want? | Yaava banna beku? |
| Which one do you want? | Nimge yaavudu beku? |
| Whose bag is this? | Idu yaara bag? |
| How much is this? | Idu eshtu? |
| How many do you want? | Eshtu beku? |
| Where are you coming from? | Neevu ellinda baruttiddiri? |
| Where are you going? | Neevu ellige hoguttiddiri? |

**Remember:**

- **Yaava** – Which, used before a noun. Example: Yaava banna? (Which colour?)
- **Yaavudu** – Which one? Example: Nimge yaavudu beku? (Which one do you want?)
- **Yaara** – Whose? Example: Idu yaara bag? (Whose bag is this?)

## 4. Mini Conversation Practice

### Conversation – Asking for Directions

**Person A:** Anna, bus nildana ellide?  
*Where is the bus station?*

**Person B:** Neravaagi hogi, balakke tirugi.  
*Go straight and turn right.*

**Person A:** Illinda eshtu doora ide?  
*How far is it from here?*

**Person B:** Ondu kilometer ide.  
*It is one kilometre away.*

**Person A:** Alli nadedu hogabahuda?  
*Can I walk there?*

**Person B:** Howdu, hogabahudu.  
*Yes, you can.*

## 🗣️ Today's Speaking Challenge

Ask a family member these five questions in Kannada:

1. Nimma hesaru enu? – What is your name?
2. Neevu elli vaasisuttiri? – Where do you live?
3. Neevu yaavaga baruttiri? – When will you come?
4. Idu enu? – What is this?
5. Idu yaara phone? – Whose phone is this?

**Challenge:** Try asking at least three new questions in Kannada during your day.
