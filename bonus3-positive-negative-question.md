# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Bonus Lesson 3
## Everyday Sentence Formation – Positive, Negative & Questions

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 10–12 minutes  
**Course:** Spoken Kannada for Beginners

---

## 🎯 Today's Goal

Learn how to form three types of everyday sentences:

1. Positive sentences – I want water.
2. Negative sentences – I don't want water.
3. Questions – Do you want water?

## 1. Positive Sentences

A positive sentence expresses something that is wanted, happening or true.

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| I want water. | Nanage neeru beku. |
| I want coffee. | Nanage kaafi beku. |
| I am going home. | Naanu manege hoguttiddene. |
| I am learning Kannada. | Naanu Kannada kaliyuttiddene. |
| He is coming. | Avanu baruttiddane. |
| She is going. | Avalu hoguttiddale. |

### Useful Pattern 1

**Nanage + Thing + Beku**

Use this pattern to say what you want or need.

Examples:
- Nanage neeru beku. – I want water.
- Nanage oota beku. – I want food.
- Nanage kaafi beku. – I want coffee.

## 2. Negative Sentences

A negative sentence expresses something you don't want or something that is not happening.

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| I don't want water. | Nanage neeru beda. |
| I don't want coffee. | Nanage kaafi beda. |
| I don't know. | Nanage gottilla. |
| I don't understand. | Nanage artha aagalla. |
| I am not going. | Naanu hoguttilla. |
| He is not coming. | Avanu baruttilla. |

### Useful Pattern 2

**Nanage + Thing + Beda**

Use this pattern to say what you don't want.

Examples:
- Nanage tea beda. – I don't want tea.
- Nanage sugar beda. – I don't want sugar.
- Nanage khaara beda. – I don't want spicy food.

**Remember:**
- Beku = Want / Need
- Beda = Don't want / No need
- Gottu = Know
- Gottilla = Don't know

## 3. Question Sentences

You can turn many simple statements into questions by using **-a?** or the appropriate question form.

| English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|
| Do you want water? | Nimge neeru beka? |
| Do you want coffee? | Nimge kaafi beka? |
| Is there a shop? | Angadi ideya? |
| Is another colour available? | Bere banna ideya? |
| Do you know Kannada? | Nimge Kannada gottideya? |
| Are you coming? | Neevu baruttiddira? |

### Useful Pattern 3

**Nimge + Thing + Beka?**

Examples:
- Nimge neeru beka? – Do you want water?
- Nimge oota beka? – Do you want food?
- Nimge coffee beka? – Do you want coffee?

## 4. Compare the Three Sentence Types

Notice how the same idea changes when we make it positive, negative or a question.

| Type | English | Kannada Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | I want water. | Nanage neeru beku. |
| Negative | I don't want water. | Nanage neeru beda. |
| Question | Do you want water? | Nimge neeru beka? |
| Positive | I want coffee. | Nanage kaafi beku. |
| Negative | I don't want coffee. | Nanage kaafi beda. |
| Question | Do you want coffee? | Nimge kaafi beka? |

## 5. Mini Conversation Practice

### Conversation – Offering Coffee

**Person A:** Nimge kaafi beka?  
*Do you want coffee?*

**Person B:** Howdu, nanage kaafi beku.  
*Yes, I want coffee.*

**Person A:** Nimge sakkare beka?  
*Do you want sugar?*

**Person B:** Beda, nanage sakkare beda.  
*No, I don't want sugar.*

**Person A:** Haalu beka?  
*Do you want milk?*

**Person B:** Howdu, swalpa haalu beku.  
*Yes, I want a little milk.*

### Section A – Fill in the Blanks

3. Nimge oota ______? (Do you want food?)
4. Nanage Kannada ______. (I don't know Kannada.)
5. Naanu manege ______. (I am going home.)
