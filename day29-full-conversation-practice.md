# 🇮🇳 Spoken Kannada – Day 29

## Full Conversation Practice (ಪೂರ್ಣ ಸಂಭಾಷಣೆ ಅಭ್ಯಾಸ)

**Level:** Beginner  
**Duration:** 20–25 minutes

### 🎯 Today's Goal

Practise complete, practical conversations in Kannada using expressions learned throughout the 30-day course.

Today's focus is on speaking naturally rather than memorising individual sentences.

### 1. Conversation 1 – Meeting Someone

**Person A:** Namaskaara! Hegiddira?  
Hello! How are you?

**Person B:** Naanu chennagiddene. Neevu hegiddiri?  
I am fine. How are you?

**Person A:** Naanu chennagiddene. Nimma hesaru enu?  
I am fine. What is your name?

**Person B:** Nanna hesaru Ravi. Nimma hesaru enu?  
My name is Ravi. What is your name?

**Person A:** Nanna hesaru Arun. Neevu ellinda bandiddiri?  
My name is Arun. Where are you from?

**Person B:** Naanu Bhatkal-dinda bandiddene.  
I am from Bhatkal.

### 2. Conversation 2 – At a Restaurant

**Customer:** Namaskaara! Menu kodi.  
Hello! Please give me the menu.

**Waiter:** Sari, illi menu ide.  
Okay, here is the menu.

**Customer:** Nanage ondu dose mattu ondu coffee beku.  
I want one dosa and one coffee.

**Waiter:** Bere enadaru beka?  
Would you like anything else?

**Customer:** Swalpa neeru kodi.  
Please give me some water.

**Customer:** Bill kodi.  
Please give me the bill.

### 3. Conversation 3 – Shopping

**Customer:** Tomato ideya?  
Do you have tomatoes?

**Shopkeeper:** Howdu, ide. Eshtu beku?  
Yes, we do. How much do you need?

**Customer:** Ondu kilo kodi.  
Please give me one kilogram.

**Customer:** Idara bele eshtu?  
How much does this cost?

**Shopkeeper:** Ondu kiloge 40 roopayi.  
40 rupees per kilogram.

**Customer:** Swalpa kadime maadi.  
Please reduce the price a little.

**Shopkeeper:** Sari, 35 roopayi kodi.  
Okay, give me 35 rupees.

**Customer:** Sari, idannu kodi.  
Okay, give me this.

### 4. Conversation 4 – Asking for Directions

**Person A:** Excuse me, bus nildana ellide?  
Excuse me, where is the bus stop?

**Person B:** Neravaagi hogi.  
Go straight.

**Person A:** Aamele?  
Then?

**Person B:** Balakke tirugi. Bus nildana alli ide.  
Turn right. The bus stop is there.

**Person A:** Tumba dhanyavaadagalu.  
Thank you very much.

**Person B:** Paravaagilla.  
No problem.

### 5. Conversation 5 – Talking on the Phone

**Person A:** Hello, yaaru maataduttiddiri?  
Hello, who is speaking?

**Person B:** Naanu Ravi maataduttiddene.  
This is Ravi speaking.

**Person A:** Ravi iddaara?  
Is Ravi there?

**Person B:** Avaru eega busy iddaare.  
He is busy now.

**Person A:** Sari, naanu nantara kare maaduttene.  
Okay, I will call later.

**Person B:** Sari, dhanyavaadagalu.  
Okay, thank you.

### 6. Conversation 6 – Asking for Help

**Person A:** Nanage swalpa sahaaya beku.  
I need a little help.

**Person B:** Enu samasye?  
What is the problem?

**Person A:** Nanage daari gottilla.  
I don't know the way.

**Person B:** Elli hogabeku?  
Where do you need to go?

**Person A:** Aspatrege hogabeku.  
I need to go to the hospital.

**Person B:** Sari, naanu daari torisuttene.  
Okay, I will show you the way.

**Person A:** Tumba dhanyavaadagalu.  
Thank you very much.
